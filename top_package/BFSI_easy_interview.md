## Caution list (harder than reputation suggests, or comp ceiling too low)
- **Zerodha** — small team, very selective despite "chill" branding
- **CRED, Razorpay, PhonePe** — among the toughest bars in Indian fintech
- **NBFCs (Bajaj Finance, ICICI Lombard, etc.)** — easy interviews but comp often below 35 LPA target for pure backend SDE
